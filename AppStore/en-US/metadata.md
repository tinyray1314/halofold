## Description

Keep working while background activity stays visible at the top of your Mac.

Halofold is a top-of-screen status hub for background work. Its first data source presents local Codex conversations as clear running, completed, and interrupted states beside the MacBook notch and in the menu bar. Completion and interruption alerts can use a system voice or your own local audio file, so you do not need to keep switching windows to check progress.

Key features:

- See running, completed, and interrupted Codex tasks
- Use a compact top-of-screen layout on both notched and non-notched displays
- View today's local token activity and official Codex weekly usage synchronized from local Codex events
- Open a task directly in the Codex app
- Reorder modules and customize layout density and alert audio
- Optionally launch when you sign in to your Mac

Privacy by design:

The app accesses your `.codex` folder only after you select it and grant read-only permission. All processing happens locally. It does not upload your task data or read or store Codex sign-in credentials.

This is an independently developed third-party app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. Codex is a trademark of its respective owner.
