# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

## Overview

This app is a local Mac utility for viewing Codex task status. It is designed around data minimization.

## Local data access

The app accesses your local `.codex` folder only after you explicitly select it. Access is read-only and is used to display task states, conversation titles, local token activity, and usage snapshots. When you explicitly use Find Tasks, the app also analyzes the text of standard conversations from the past seven days on your Mac to generate task candidates for your review. The app does not read, copy, or store Codex sign-in credentials.

Folder authorization is stored as a macOS security-scoped bookmark inside the app's own container so that the app can continue working after it restarts. You can revoke this access by uninstalling the app and removing its container data.

## Data collection and transmission

The app does not collect personal data, use analytics or advertising SDKs, track users, or transmit the contents of your `.codex` folder to the developer or any third party.

Settings, task-reading checkpoints, deduplication identifiers for imported tasks, and alert audio files that you explicitly record or import remain in the app container on your Mac. Task deduplication identifiers do not contain full conversation text.

When you explicitly use recording-to-text, the app requires macOS to use available on-device Chinese speech recognition. The app does not download a local AI voice pack by default.

## Third-party services

The app does not include third-party data collection services. When you select a task, the app uses a local `codex://` link to open the installed Codex app.

## Children's privacy

The app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices materially change, this policy and its last-updated date will be revised, with appropriate disclosure in the app or its store listing.
